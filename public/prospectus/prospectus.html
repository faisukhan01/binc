Welcome

A campus that feels like family

Bright International College, Township, Lahore — where professional degrees meet personal attention.

Bright International College (BINC) is a career-focused institution in the heart of GECHS Township, Lahore, offering three high-demand professional degrees: Pharm-D, Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) and BS Computer Science (BSCS). Our mission is simple — make quality professional education accessible, affordable and genuinely career-building for every student who walks through our gates.

Small classes mean our faculty knows every student by name. Modern laboratories, structured hospital rotations and industry mentorship ensure that learning never stays limited to textbooks. Whether you dream of wearing a pharmacist's coat, running a rehabilitation clinic, or building the next great software product, Bright gives you the environment, the mentoring and the confidence to get there.

We are equally proud of our social welfare commitment: through the welfare foundation (PWWF), eligible welfare workers and their families receive complete admission support — because we believe financial circumstances should never stand between a hardworking family and a professional education.

Programs Offered

Choose your professional degree

Three career-focused programs — each combining strong academics with practical, real-world training.

5 YEARS

Pharm-D

Doctor of Pharmacy

Pharm-D is a five-year professional doctorate that trains you to become a licensed pharmacy professional — from the science behind medicines to hands-on patient care in hospitals and community pharmacies. Graduates step into one of healthcare's most trusted and consistently in-demand professions.

Who can apply

  • Intermediate (FSc Pre-Medical) or equivalent qualification
  • Original academic documents — Matric & Intermediate certificates
  • CNIC / B-Form and recent passport-size photographs

The five-year journey

Year 1Anatomy, physiology & pharmaceutical chemistry foundations
Year 2Pharmacology & pharmaceutics — how drugs work and are formulated
Year 3Pharmaceutical technology & medicinal chemistry
Year 4Clinical, hospital & community pharmacy practice
Year 5Clinical rotations, research project & professional internship

Career pathways

Community PharmacistHospital PharmacistPharma IndustryResearch
5 YEARS

DPT

Doctor of Physical Therapy

DPT is a five-year clinical doctorate focused on physical rehabilitation. Students learn to assess, diagnose and treat movement disorders through evidence-based therapy — in hospitals, sports settings and private practice. Training combines modern rehabilitation laboratories with supervised clinical placements from the early years onward.

Who can apply

  • Intermediate (FSc Pre-Medical) or equivalent qualification
  • Original academic documents — Matric & Intermediate certificates
  • CNIC / B-Form and recent passport-size photographs

The five-year journey

Year 1Basic medical sciences — anatomy, physiology & kinesiology
Year 2Pathology, pharmacology & biomechanics
Year 3Therapeutic exercise, electrotherapy & assessment skills
Year 4Musculoskeletal, neuro & cardiopulmonary rehabilitation
Year 5Supervised clinical internships & research project

Career pathways

Clinical DPTSports RehabRehab CentersPrivate Practice
4 YEARS

BSCS

BS Computer Science

BSCS is a four-year computing degree built around real-world projects. From programming fundamentals to modern artificial intelligence and data science, the program blends strong theory with practical labs, industry mentorship and a final-year project that becomes the centrepiece of your professional portfolio.

Who can apply

  • Intermediate (ICS / FSc / ICom / DAE) or equivalent qualification
  • Original academic documents — Matric & Intermediate certificates
  • CNIC / B-Form and recent passport-size photographs

The four-year journey

Year 1Programming fundamentals, mathematics & IT essentials
Year 2Data structures, OOP & database systems
Year 3Software engineering, operating systems, networks & AI
Year 4Specialization electives, final-year project & internship

Career pathways

Software EngineerAI / Data ScienceCyber SecurityEntrepreneurship
Welfare & Scholarships

Education support that changes lives

100% Admission FREE for eligible welfare workers

In partnership with the social welfare foundation (PWWF), Bright International College offers complete admission support to eligible welfare workers and their families. This is not a partial discount — it is full support through the admission journey, reflecting our belief that those who serve the community deserve a door into professional education.

Our welfare desk verifies eligibility personally and walks each approved family through every step — documentation, admission and enrollment — with zero stress and zero hidden conditions.

How to Apply

Four simple steps to your seat

The whole journey takes less than two minutes to start — and our team guides you through the rest.

01

Apply Online

Fill the online admission form at www.binc.edu.pk with your details and preferred program. It takes less than two minutes, and you receive a tracking code instantly.

02

Admissions Team Calls You

Our admissions office contacts you on WhatsApp or phone to verify your details, answer questions and guide the next steps — usually the same day.

03

Submit Documents

Bring your academic documents, CNIC/B-Form and photographs to the campus — or send clear scans to us on WhatsApp for pre-verification.

04

Confirm Your Seat

Pay the admission dues, receive your fee schedule and welcome kit — and your Bright journey officially begins.

Campus & Facilities

Where learning comes alive

From the first semester, BINC students learn in spaces designed for practice: a pharmacy lab for hands-on dispenser training, a rehabilitation gym for DPT clinical practice, modern computer labs for coding sessions and AI projects, and a quiet library for study and research. Beyond the classrooms, campus life is warm and community-driven — convocation celebrations, society events and mentorship circles that last well beyond graduation.

Common Questions

Frequently asked

When do Fall 2026 admissions open and close?

Admissions are open now. Seats per program are limited and filled first-come, merit basis — early applicants secure their seats and documents verification comfortably ahead of the session start in September 2026.

Is the admission really free for welfare workers?

Yes. Eligible social welfare workers registered under the welfare foundation (PWWF) receive 100% free admission support. WhatsApp 0324-3334977 and the welfare desk will verify your eligibility and handle the rest.

Can I get the fee structure before applying?

Absolutely. Submit the inquiry form or WhatsApp 0324-3334977 and the admissions team will share the complete fee schedule, installment options and any welfare support you qualify for.

Where exactly is the campus located?

57 Sector A, GECHS Township, near Pindi Stop, Lahore — easily reachable from all parts of the city, with the admissions office open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Which programs are offered at Bright International College?

We currently offer Pharm-D (Doctor of Pharmacy, 5 Years), DPT (Doctor of Physical Therapy, 5 Years) and BSCS (BS Computer Science, 4 Years) — all career-focused degrees with practical training built in.

What documents are required for admission?

Your last academic transcripts/certificates (Matric & Intermediate), CNIC or B-Form, and recent passport-size photographs. Originals are verified at the campus before enrollment.
